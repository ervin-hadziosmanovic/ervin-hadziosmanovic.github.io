I am a Ph.D student at Scuola Normale Superiore in Pisa, under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://people.dm.unipi.it/frigerio/"> Roberto Frigerio </a>.

Before that, I obtained my master's degree at the University of Pavia under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://mate.unipv.it/ghigi/">Alessandro Ghigi </a>.
 
I am interested in bounded cohomology, simplicial volume and geometric group theory.
 
From February to May 2026, I visited Prof. <a href="https://alexsisto.net/">Alessandro Sisto </a> and the <a href="https://www.macs.hw.ac.uk/~lc45/Conferences/MAXIMALS.html"> GGT group</a> at Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh.

Together with other Ph.D students from the University of Pisa and the Scuola Normale Superiore, I am organising the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/babygeometri">Baby Geometri Seminar</a> in Pisa.

I am also co-organising the fourth edition of <a href="https://crmdegiorgi.sns.it/evento/lit-iv-landing-in-topology/"> LiT: Landing in Topology</a> in Pisa, a workshop aimed at young researches in low dimensional topology and geometric group theory.
